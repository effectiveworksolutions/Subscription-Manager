## Full description

Streaming, music, cloud storage, AI tools, the gym — subscriptions add up fast and most of us have no idea what the real monthly total is. SubTracker shows you.

**See the real number**
Every subscription on one screen with your true monthly and yearly spend. Yearly plans are shown as a monthly equivalent so you can compare apples to apples.

**Never get caught out**
Enter one past billing date and SubTracker works out every future renewal automatically. Turn on reminders and get a notification before each charge hits your card.

**Spot the waste**
The Insights tab breaks spending down by category, ranks your biggest costs, and shows exactly what's coming out over the next 30 days.

**Private by design**
No ads, no trackers, no analytics. Your data lives on your device. Optional free sync keeps your phone and computer in step, locked to your account and nobody else's.

**Your data, your way**
Export to a spreadsheet or back up to a file at any time. Delete everything with one tap.

Quick-pick logos for Netflix, Stan, Disney+, Binge, Kayo, Spotify, YouTube Premium, HBO Max, Paramount+, Apple, Microsoft 365, ChatGPT, Claude and more. Or add anything custom.

Free. No account required.
