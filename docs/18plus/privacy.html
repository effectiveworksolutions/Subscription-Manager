Privacy Policy

SubTracker · Last updated 3 October 2026

SubTracker is a personal subscription tracker. This policy explains what information the app handles and what happens to it. The short version: your data is yours, we don't sell it, and we only store what's needed to make the app work.

What the app stores

  • Subscription details you enter — service name, price, billing cycle, status, start date, payment method label (e.g. "Visa 1234"), a link to the service, and notes.
  • App preferences — whether reminders are on, how many days before to remind, and whether you've seen the welcome screen.
  • Your email address and a password — only if you choose to create an account for cloud sync.
  • Reminder preferences — whether you want email reminders and how many days before a renewal, if you turn them on.
  • Where a subscription was found — if you use "Find subscriptions from your email", the address of the mailbox each imported subscription came from (e.g. you@gmail.com) and the last four digits of the card shown on the receipt, if any.

SubTracker never asks for, and has no way to receive, full card numbers, bank credentials, or passwords for other services.

Where it's stored

  • On your device. Everything is saved locally in your browser or app storage. You can use SubTracker entirely offline without an account.
  • In your private cloud account (optional). If you sign in, your subscriptions are copied to a database hosted by Supabase in Sydney, Australia, so they sync between your devices. Row-level security means only your authenticated account can read or change your rows.

What we don't do

  • No advertising, no ad networks, no trackers.
  • No analytics SDKs. We don't track how you use the app.
  • No selling or sharing of your data with third parties.
  • No access to your contacts, photos, location, or other apps.

Third-party services

  • Supabase (authentication and database, optional) — privacy policy.
  • Google Fonts (typography) — fonts are loaded from Google's CDN, which may log your IP address. Google privacy policy.
  • Service logos — the app shows each service's icon by requesting it from Google's favicon service (or DuckDuckGo's as a fallback). Those requests include the service's website address but nothing about you or your subscription. Logos are cached on your device.
  • Google and Microsoft sign-in (finding subscriptions from email, optional) — if you connect Gmail or Outlook, their sign-in page runs in your browser and their mail APIs are called directly from your device. Google privacy policy · Microsoft privacy statement.
  • Resend (email delivery, optional) — if you turn on email reminders, a daily message listing your upcoming renewals is sent to your email address through Resend. Resend privacy policy.
  • GitHub Pages (web hosting) — standard server logs. GitHub privacy statement.

Notifications and email reminders

If you turn on reminders, the app schedules notifications on your device for upcoming renewals. These are generated locally and nothing is sent to a server to make them work. If you also turn on email reminders (requires an account), our server reads your list once a day to work out what is due and emails you a summary. Turn it off in Settings at any time.

Shared lists

You can invite other people to co-own your list. Everyone on a shared list can see, add, edit and delete every subscription on it, and can see each other's email address in Settings. Invite codes expire after 14 days. The list owner can remove members, and members can leave, at any time. Only share a list with people you trust.

Finding subscriptions from your email

If you choose Find subscriptions from your email, SubTracker asks Google or Microsoft for read-only access to that mailbox through their own sign-in page. We never see, ask for or store your email password. The app then searches the last 12 months for receipts, renewal and trial emails and reads only those messages — on your device, in your browser. Nothing from your mailbox is sent to our servers or to anyone else, and the emails themselves are not kept.

You review what was found and tick what to add. Only the resulting subscription details are saved to your list: service name, price, billing cycle, date of the last charge, the mailbox it came from and, if the receipt shows it, the last four digits of the card. The access token is held in memory only and is revoked when the scan finishes or you close the sheet. You can also remove SubTracker's access at any time from your Google Account or Microsoft account.

SubTracker's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Pasting a receipt email works the same way: the text is read on your device and discarded once the subscription is added.

Check my inbox (in "Ask about this subscription") uses the same read-only access for a single, narrower search: emails from that one service, or naming it, in the last six months. It reports the latest receipt, any cancellation or failed-payment notice, and which mailbox they were in, then offers to update that one entry. Access is switched off as soon as the check finishes, and nothing from the emails is kept beyond the details you choose to apply.

AI review and "Ask about this subscription"

The "AI review" feature compares the subscriptions on your list against a set of rules about overlapping services, bundles and plan types. "Ask about this subscription" answers questions (is it active, which email, next charge, what you've paid, how to cancel) from the details already on your list using the same kind of rules. Both run entirely on your device; nothing is sent to any AI service.

Your control

  • Export — Settings → Back up to file gives you everything as JSON or CSV at any time.
  • Delete — Settings → Delete all data removes everything from your device and, if signed in, from your cloud account (deleted rows are kept as "tombstones" for up to 60 days so your other devices learn about the deletion, then purged).
  • Close your account — delete all data, then email us and we'll remove the account record itself within 7 days.

Adults-only services (18+ edition)

This edition of SubTracker can list adult entertainment services in its service picker. They are hidden until you confirm in the app that you are 18 or older; that confirmation is stored only on your device and can be switched off again in Settings. Choosing one of these services only records its name, price and renewal date on your own list, exactly like any other subscription. We never contact these services and never tell them you use SubTracker.

Children

SubTracker is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top will update and significant changes will be noted in the app.
